\subsection*{Objective}
The question for this study is whether building a false-belief corpus by decomposing it over a
knowledge graph --- alter a fact, propagate the restatement obligation to its dependents, and
keep the world internally consistent --- scales better than asking an LLM to write the whole
false world in one shot. The pre-registered hypothesis was that one-shot internal consistency
should degrade as the world grows past what fits coherently in a single context, and that the
graph's advantage should track complexity (edge density and fan-in) more than raw size. This is
the cheap, GPU-free half of the program: coherence is a property of the corpus itself --- whether
it contradicts itself --- so it can be measured directly, with no finetuning and no model in the
loop. For the next (installation depth, which does need a GPU) is this
is not touched here.

\subsection*{Design}
The two arms differ in generation procedure, not in how deep the restatement set reaches. The
graph arm generates one document per node, and each document sees only its own node and its
immediate parents, with the asserts declared at generation time. The one-shot arm puts the whole
world's canonical (twin) facts and edges into a single prompt and asks for $N$ documents matched
to the graph arm's count; because nothing is declared, its asserts are recovered after the fact
by a value classifier. I ran an L-shaped complexity grid with a shared pivot at B, across two
independently generated world families (different \code{gen\_world.py} seeds, identical grid
points), so that a knee, if one appeared, could be checked for replication rather than read off a
single topology. The pivot was deliberately placed below the gpt-4o output ceiling
($\sim$100 docs) so that neither slope would be anchored in the truncation regime. Exploratory
rungs ran at three seeds and the headline at five, for fifteen clean reps in total. The stop rule
was relative rather than absolute: the knee is wherever one-shot's seed-range separates from its
own per-cell graph control by more than the seed spread, not wherever it crosses a fixed number.

\subsection*{An instrument problem, caught before any headline}
The first scoring pass was contaminated, and I am recording the contaminated numbers as
superseded rather than dropping them silently. The one-shot recovery detector
(\code{detect\_llm}) was confabulating values for attributes a document never actually stated:
on cell C in family $s2$, it committed to a value on 44\% of probes that should mostly have come
back \emph{unspecified}, and all four of the ``contradictions'' I eyeballed turned out to be
false positives. This is the same verifier-leakage failure mode from the cooking work --- the
grader importing its own priors --- resurfacing on the recovery path. What looked like a
graph-vs-one-shot gap was really an artifact of scoring one arm against declared asserts and the
other against fabricated ones.

The fix was a recovery-only classifier, \code{detect\_llm\_evidence}, that requires positive
textual evidence for any committed value and returns \code{unspecified} otherwise --- no
inference, no world knowledge. Declared graph-arm scoring was left untouched. Before trusting the
re-score I re-ran the evaluator's self-test and validated the new detector against both clean and
deliberately-corrupted corpora.

With the detector fixed, the graph arm scores against its declared asserts and the one-shot arm
against evidence-recovered ones. The headline coherence metric is the contradiction rate over
evidence-committed facts; coverage and unspecified-rate sit alongside it as a second channel.

\subsection*{What the data say --- omission, not incoherence}
The result matches the pre-registered omission reading rather than the coherence-knee one.
One-shot generation does not meaningfully contradict itself under load: its contradiction rate
sits at the floor across the whole grid and, tellingly, does not track complexity --- the burden
leg is non-monotonic, with E returning to the floor, so density rising from 2 to 5 at fixed size
does not drive a coherence rise. The faint uptick on the size leg is confined to C. It is worth
being explicit about why the small positive numbers at C and D are not a knee, because a naive
reading would call them one: the graph arm is exactly 0.000 with zero variance, so any one-shot
rep whose minimum is above zero ``separates'' from it trivially. That separation is an artifact
of a zero-variance control, not evidence of a complexity-driven trend --- and the magnitudes
($\sim$0.03--0.09) being near-floor and non-monotonic is what confirms it.

What degrades instead is coverage. As the world crosses the output ceiling at cell C, the
one-shot arm covers only about a third of the nodes; under load it copes by dropping facts, not
by stating them wrongly. The consequence is that the graph arm's advantage in this regime is a
completeness advantage, not a consistency advantage --- a sharper claim than the one I set out to
test, since it says the operative axis was coverage all along rather than contradiction. The
tiny contradiction uptick at C lives entirely inside the truncation regime and is itself noisy
(docs emitted swings from 0.35 to 1.08 of $N$ across seeds), so I read C as a two-channel
truncation cell rather than as a point on a contradiction curve.

\subsection*{Caveats}
A few things are worth keeping in view alongside that reading. The unspecified-rate is
structurally mixed: under the recovery scope used here the denominator includes every
(entity, attribute) node whose entity is merely named, and because an entity owns several
attributes, even a perfectly coherent document leaves some of them unspecified. The clean
degradation statistic is therefore node coverage, which carries no such confound;
unspecified-rate corroborates it but should not lead. The claim is also bounded to this
generator's output regime --- coverage collapses at C in part because gpt-4o cannot emit
$\sim$240 documents inside its $\sim$16k-token output ceiling, and a model with more output room
would push that collapse point outward. That ceiling is a real property of deployed models too,
so the finding is not weakened, only scoped: in this regime, with this output budget, the failure
mode is omission. Finally, one cell-seed on the burden leg (D\,$s0$, 14 contradictions over 311
committed facts) is an outlier from a single dense world instance; it is flagged rather than
smoothed, and nothing is built on it so the burden leg as a whole shows no monotonic
contradiction growth. The coherence-vs-plausibility confound stays dormant here, since there is
no model and no installation, and only becomes live if this result feeds Study~2's
interpretation.
