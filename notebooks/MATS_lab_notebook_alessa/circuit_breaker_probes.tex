\section{Setup and what differs from the RMU experiment}
 
The formal object is unchanged from the RMU writeup: the hiddenness $\Hid_\tau(M,T)$ of
Eq.~\eqref{eq:hid}, the minimum recovery budget to restore task-$T$ accuracy above $\tau$,
instantiated here on the \emph{representational} axis (a probe of capacity class $\mathcal{C}$
and token-aggregation $g$ reading the frozen residual stream).
\begin{equation}
\Hid_\tau(M, T) \;=\; \min\Bigl\{\, c \;:\; \Acc\bigl(\Rc_c(M), T\bigr) \ge \tau \,\Bigr\}.
\label{eq:hid}
\end{equation}

The things that differ: 

\paragraph{three models}
 The Deep Ignorance family provides a genuine
\emph{never-knew} anchor that the RMU/Zephyr setting lacked. We probe all three identically
(all full merged \texttt{GPTNeoXForCausalLM}, 32 layers, hidden $4096$, identical architecture
$\Rightarrow$ directly comparable activations):
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=2pt]
  \item $\Mknows$ = \texttt{deep-ignorance-unfiltered} --- knows-it ceiling / positive control.
  \item $\Mcb$ = \texttt{deep-ignorance-unfiltered-cb} --- the CB target.
  \item $\Mnever$ = \texttt{deep-ignorance-e2e-strong-filter} --- never-knew floor (genuine
        data-filtering removal).
\end{itemize}

So the question then becomes: \emph{where does the CB probe signal fall between $\Mknows$ (ceiling) and
$\Mnever$ (floor)?} CB near the floor $\Rightarrow$ filtering-like removal; CB near the ceiling
$\Rightarrow$ shallow rotation with knowledge intact (the failure mode hypothesized for CB);

\section{Method}
 
The probe grid, controls, and cross-validated signal ($\text{signal} = \Acc_{\text{real}} -
\Acc_{\text{shuffle}}$, the meaningful quantity in the $p\!\gg\!n$ regime) are as in the RMU
study: $\{\text{linear},\text{MLP}\}\times\{\text{last-token},\text{full-mean},\text{option-span},
\text{learned-attention}\}$ at all 33 layers, with scaler statistics fit train-fold-only and the
probe layer locked to the knows-it model's best layer. Two adaptations were required by the
weak base and are defended in \S\ref{sec:robust}: a \emph{model-relative} positive-control gate
(the RMU study's absolute $0.55$ threshold is tied to Zephyr's competence and would wrongly fail
a weaker-but-healthy model), and the exclusion of the learned-attention cell as a probe
optimization failure rather than a CB null.

\subsection{Off-readout cells lack a positive control (inconclusive, not nulls)}
On this weak base the answer signal does not survive pooling: in the full-mean, option-span, and
learned-attention cells the \emph{knows-it} positive control itself collapses to the noise floor
($+0.04$ to $+0.06$), clustering indistinguishably with CB and the never-knew model. A cell with
no working positive control cannot yield a meaningful null: if the probe cannot find signal where
signal is known to exist, its failure elsewhere is uninformative. These cells are we so far
reported as \textbf{inconclusive}, and the CB conclusion rests on the readout cells, where the
control works. (This contrasts with the RMU setting, where every grid cell carried a strong
positive control.)

\subsection{The within-model dissociation: the robust headline}

The knows-it model decodes both sets nearly equally (it knows both; tiny gap); CB shows by far
the largest forget-vs-retain separation. This also rules out the principal benign explanation of
the readout null---that the probe is simply blind to CB's (globally rotated, \S\ref{sec:mech})
residual stream: the identical probe recovers CB on retain at $+0.137 \approx \Mknows$'s $+0.167$,
failing only on the forget set. The null is content-specific.
 
\subsection{Mechanistic contrast with RMU}
\label{sec:mech}
Per-layer activation divergence between $\Mknows$ and $\Mcb$ shows: identical embeddings (layer
0), near-identical through layer 2, onset at layer 3 (cosine $0.67$), and near-orthogonal from
layer 4 onward (cosine $\approx 0$, frequently negative) through the entire stack. CB is thus a
\emph{global rotation} of the representations, consistent with its merged adapters at layers
5/10/15/20/25/30. This contrasts sharply with RMU, which remained bit-identical through layer 5
and applied more of a localized, direction-preserving steer at layers 6--7 ( with cosine staying high).
