\section{Two representational instruments}


\paragraph{Probe battery (decodability)}
Probes of capacity class $C$ (linear, MLP) and token aggregation $g$ (last-token, full-mean, option-span, learned-attention), fit per layes with 5-fold stratified cross validation. In the regime here ($d=4096 \gg n$) training accuracy is identically  $1.0$ and uninformative. The only meaningful quantity is the held-out \emph{signal} $:=\Acc_{\text{real}}-\Acc_{\text{shuffle}}$. Every result is also gated on a working positive control -- negative on a cell whose positive control does not fire is recorded as inconclusive, not as a null.

\paragraph{readout-1 (contraction geometry).} 
We have two channels on the forget-set activations, per layer. (i) Effective rank via participation ratio 
$\PR = (\sum_i\lambda_i)^2 / \sum_i\lambda_i^2$ on the covariance eigenvalues
$\lambda_i$ which functions as a soft count of how many directions the activation cloud actually occupies
(even spread over $k$ directions $\Rightarrow \PR\approx k$; collapse onto one $\Rightarrow \PR\to 1$). MMD to a per-cloud moment-matched Gaussian --- a
scale-normalized index of distributional \emph{shape} (anisotropy, correlation), so
absolute mean/scale offsets between models drop out. PR is the scale-sensitive primary;
MMD corroborates and, being within-model and scale-normalized, is the cleaner channel when
two models differ in overall rank.

Some shared control/experimental design accross: 

Train-fold-only scaler statistics; selectivity always checked against a retain set (MMLU-bio) the model was not asked to forget; matched $N$ subsampling whenever two clouds od different size are compared on PR/MMD ((both are
$N$-dependent --- PR is hard-capped near $N{-}1$, MMD$^2$ carries finite-sample positive
bias). And the instruments are validtaed against a positive control known to fire before any negative is trusted. 

\section{Method-by-method summary}
\subsection{RMU (Zephyr-7B)}
Probe Battery: a grid-wide null. No probe recovers the WMDP answer at any of the 33 layers in any of the eight (capacity $\times$ aggregation) cells, while identical probes recover $\sim\!0.6$ from base everywhere. The null survives a pathology audit (probes converge); activation norms within n $\sim\!15\%$ of base; held-out accuracy
$\equiv$ shuffle at every layer) and is selective: on MMLU-bio retain the RMU model decodes
as well as base. readout-1: a sharp $\PR$ collapse ($-94\%$) localized to the documented
steer layer $L7$, forget-specific. \textbf{Regime: present, hidden by contraction;
representationally dead (decodability) at the tested budgets, contraction localized.}


\subsection{Circuit Breakers (Deep Ignorance GPT-NeoX)}
In this case: a three-model design (knows-it/CB/never-knew) 
This replaces RMU's chance anchor with a real ceiling and floor. Probe battery:  the readout cells (the only ones with a working positive
control on this weak $0.43$-competent base) show no WMDP-bio recovery; off-readout cells
are \emph{inconclusive} (positive control collapses), not nulls. The robust headline is a
within-model forget-vs-retain decodability dissociation. readout-1: forget $\PR$ collapses
to $\sim\!2$--$4$ across $L4$--$30$ with a forget-specific MMD spike -- - \emph{contraction},
not the rank-preserving rotation that was pre-registered (see the individual entry for the
falsification). Then we have decodaility at the collapse site: the forget answer is  is \emph{not} linearly
recoverable, at the never-knew floor, capacity no help. Regime: present, hidden by
contraction; representationally dead at the collapse site; broad multi-layer collapse
(distinct from RMU's sharp $L7$).

\subsection{RepNoise (Llama-2-7b-chat)}
In this case we have a harmful-finetuniung defense(MMD-toward-noise on BeaverTails) tested on its own forget domain at is loss's own site  (MMD-toward-noise on BeaverTails), tested on its own forget
domain at its loss's own site (MLP-output, answer span). readout-1: \emph{no} contraction
signature --- flat PR, no MMD collapse, sub-1\% weight movement, changes symmetric across
harmful/harmless. The instrument was validated against RMU (it fired) before the negative
was trusted; a nominally stronger $\beta{=}4$ checkpoint was rejected as a positive control
after a weight-diff showed it moved weights \emph{less} than the default. \textbf{Regime:
banked scoped negative: no RMU-like contraction for these released checkpoints, at this
site.}

\subsection{Deep Ignorance never-knew anchor}
Since this our data-filtered model it gives us the genuine-absence reference the whole "destroyed vs hidden" question requires.  readout-1: forget $\PR$ \emph{intact} (tens, tracking the knows-it
model), no MMD spike, $\approx$zero forget-vs-retain gap. Genuine absence reads as
\emph{flat}, not collapsed --- the counterintuitive but essential point: not knowing
something does not make activations low-rank, because the model still runs full-dimensional
language computation over the prompt. \textbf{Regime: genuinely absent.}

\paragraph{What the axis established.}
Genuine absence has a measurable signature, and it is ``flat,'' not ``collapsed.' The never-knew anchor gives ``hidden but present'' something to look anomalous against. This is a useful reference frame for the project.
